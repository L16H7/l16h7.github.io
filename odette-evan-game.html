How to Play

You are Odette. Click squares to paint them red.

After each move, Evan checks whether a subset of red squares can be recolored blue so every row & column has an even number of blue squares.

Odette's optimal strategy survives up to 4n−1 squares.

When does Evan win?

Evan wins whenever any subset of red squares covers each of its rows and columns an even number of times.
